## 3. What changed this round

**Engine (engine and method 1.1.1, pipeline 1.1.1)**
- **Light from the pixels (21).** The serve scan measures mains flicker and a dark sky: flicker makes the class `flicker`, and a near-black sky over a lit court turns a daylight answer into court lights. Pixels never raise the class. Renders: 5 of 5 night and 5 of 5 flicker caught, 0 false calls on the rest (VALIDATION §3.6).
- **Caught tosses are not serves (23).** Counting needs contact evidence. Production build: still 45 of 45 serves counted, 0 false serves.
- **Only a trusted contact rejects a toss apex (36).**
- **Toss height is experimental (41).** V1 now also needs the method-only (truth-landmark) variant to pass. Absolute toss height fails it (median 4.1%, p95 5.6% of height), so it is experimental everywhere; its spread and each toss against the player's own middle toss stay Estimate.
- **Per-serve scale (E1).** A serve with too few standing frames borrows its standing height from serves at the same distance, and a serve whose toss falls at a different rate is flagged, never rescaled. Serve 7 of the session clip: contact height 1.48 × height (truth 1.50; was 1.71).
- **Recording dates (E2).** The probe reads the container's recording time, else the file's last change (not a copy made in the last 5 minutes), else the import time; the Import screen's "When was it filmed?" lets the player change the day; coaching gets the session's own date.

**Coaching and data (coaching 1.1.1)**
- **Pain (0).** The newest pain or sore log, from any drill, holds every drill's harder version; only goal-met logs after it count towards an unlock.
- **History window (12).** A practice or match dated today counts at once; "Welcome back" gives the earlier weekly number; after a break the session budget starts at the first week's number.
- **Delete everything (18)** also forgets analysis jobs, the Record flow and Today's dismissed card.
- **Baseline note (20)** counts only matched sessions with enough usable serves.
- **Blocks (C1).** Each recording of a visit is its own block (schema v3); the sample's blocks are numbered on load.

**UI and copy**
- **Experimental data never picks the focus (44)**, even with the toggle on; it can only be listed under "Also noticed", marked experimental (COACHING §5.1, §13; DECISIONS D2).
- **Welcome strip (48)** is capped by the current catalogue: toss offsets from the middle toss, never metres.
- **Sample (49)** rebuilt by engine 1.1.1, with a test that fails if any stored status or level is above what the catalogue allows.
- **Leg drive (50)** carries "reads about 2.5% of height low; compare with your own serves only" wherever it appears.
- **How we measure (55)** no longer overstates the validated scope.
- **Tilt copy (66)** says the tilt correction has not been checked on real phones.
- **Small screens and large text (70, 71, 74, 76):** the value drawer, the Check-this-serve Save bar, the tagging pad and live framing were re-laid out and checked at 320 to 430 px wide, in landscape, and at 150% and 200% text.
- **Beta unlock and Plus preview (U1).** Everything is unlocked for beta testers; T1, T2 and T3 show a non-blocking preview with the proposed price and one question, "Would you pay A$99.99 a year for this?", stored as the local event `plus_wtp_answered` (PRODUCT §5.3, MONETISATION §6).
- **Radar row (U2).** The session report shows "Radar speed (you logged)" with its source, from a reading entered in Check this serve.
- **Match log entry points (U3).** Today's morning-after card (the day after a comp night; dismissible) and Profile → Match log.
