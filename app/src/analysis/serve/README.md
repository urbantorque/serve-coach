# Serve layer (engine-core)

Serve finding, rectification, left/right relabelling, events and phases (METRICS §1.1, §2). Pure TypeScript; no DOM.

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `geometry.ts` | Camera model: long-side FOV mapped to the displayed orientation (26 mm: 67.3°, f_HU 0.751 portrait), pitch/roll from `CaptureContext.orientationSamples`, the level rotation `H = K·R·K⁻¹` (5° cap; measured rear tilt up to 25°), camera distance `D = f_HU·H_m/H_HU`, set-up class. Imports (METRICS §1.1 item 3a): the side-view mount answer is a prior, never a correction (`assumed-level`, pitch and roll 0 ± 2° for a level mount, ± 4° roughly level; `MOUNT_TILT_SIGMA_DEG`); `pitchRatioPerDeg` (Δr = r(1 − r)·H/D per radian: 1.2 cm/° at a 3.3 m peak from 7.5 m, 1.7 cm/° for a 3.5 m peak from 6 m) and `rollRatioPerDeg` give the height error; `bodyAxisRollDeg` is the standing-body roll check (> 3° = the answer is not trusted). |
| `track.ts` | Compact rectified pose track; landmark access with visibility floors; mid-hip, lowest foot point, head top; local quadratic values and velocities (never frame differences). |
| `relabel.ts` | The mirror fix. Two-state Viterbi per limb group (upper, lower): transitions from limb continuity, evidence from the entered handedness (tossing hand nearest the ball around release, highest wrist at contact, front foot towards the net in stance). On the corpus the mirrored share of side-view frames drops from 37% to 0% (median). |
| `windows.ts` | `findServeWindows`: label-free anchors on the higher wrist (≥ 0.95 BH, ≥ nose + 0.08, prominence ≥ 0.30 BH, a toss of ≥ 0.85 BH before), merged within 1.2 s, windows [t − 2.2, t + 2.0] s split at midpoints. Works on sparse (15–30 Hz) poses: the height bars drop by the most a short contact peak can hide between samples (`samplingAllowance`, K·(gap/2)², K = 50 BH/s², capped at 0.12 BH) and local maxima are judged within 0.03 BH. Pass A simulation on the corpus: 100% recall at every 15 Hz phase (was 98.0%), no extra windows. |
| `ball.ts` | Per-window toss track, lead-in, `analyseToss` on rectified observations, the §2.4.3 apex case (bracketed, fitted above the frame, censored, too few), `ball-intersection` and `ball-bracket` contact. |
| `events.ts` | Generic fusion rule; measured noise per serve (§5.5); release (hand method as a lag-corrected fallback only), trophy (lowest hip, flatness-based σ), wrist apex, the label-free swing peak (`swingPeakTime`: the highest wrist in the last 0.25 s of the last arm-up stretch that reaches the swing's height, for the fused-event check), motion and setup start, landing (per-foot standing level), recovery end. |
| `analyse.ts` | One window → `ServeCore`: frame of reference (floor row, upright body height), roles, events, classification (serve / retoss / shadow / other, NO_CONTACT), S0. Calibrated bias terms live in `CALIBRATION` (development seeds s1–s3). **Fused-event check (DECISIONS D2 addendum)**, `checkContactSignals`: the ball (intersection and bracket, one signal), the hit sound and the swing peak (found on its own, not around the other estimates) are compared pairwise at 3 combined σ (at least a frame). A majority of three drops the outlier; a spread fuses all three with a σ covering it; a ball seen *leaving* (outgoing track meeting the toss path) against one disagreeing signal keeps the ball; in each of these, every contact-dependent value is capped at experimental (`CONTACT_NOT_FOUND`). Anything else (e.g. a ball that only vanished from its path against the swing peak) is `unresolved`: contact confidence 0.45 and every dependent value withheld. On the corpus (129 serves, `metrics-eval`, hit sound off): 2 disputes, both dusk clips where MediaPipe missed the hitting arm; `dusk-s1` experimental, `dusk-s3` withheld (its ball evidence alone had confidence below 0.5). None on the 45 serves of the production end-to-end run (`validation/results/e2e-final/`). The swing peak is the highest wrist in the last 0.25 s of the last arm-up stretch that reaches within 0.08 BH of the swing's highest point, so a lower blip in the follow-through is not taken for it. **Counting**: a serve counts from the swing plus toss evidence even when the toss could not be fully analysed (the toss track rising ≥ 1.35 BH, or the ball layer's own fit when the track was cut before its apex); its ball-dependent values then carry their own reasons. A shadow swing (no ball) and a dribble (peak below 1.35 BH) are not counted. |
| `phases.ts` | The seven product phases with key frames (§2.6). |

Validation: `validation/results/metrics/SUMMARY.md` (tools/validate/metrics-eval.ts).
